About

Data, automation, and a few thousand photographs.

Most days that means designing recognition and recast models across Order-to-Earnings, teaching agents to catch exceptions before people have to, and rebuilding old Alteryx pipelines as Python that someone else can actually maintain. Photography is the other half of how I pay attention to the world around me. It's less about the finished picture and more about the habit of noticing the small moments that matter.
