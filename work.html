Data analyst · PwC

DAT Tech & Data · Revenue Automation · Silicon Valley

I work in revenue automation within PwC’s DAT Tech & Data team in Silicon Valley. My work connects revenue models, data validation, and the tools used to run those processes.

Project example

Revenue recast for audit preparation

The client needed a revenue recast across a large volume of booking and invoice records in preparation for an audit.

What I built
A custom solution that brought the booking and invoice records together, processed the recast, and reconciled the data.
What it produced
Journal entries and financial information prepared for the client’s audit.
  • 01

    Revenue models

    I build revenue-recognition and recast models, check business logic against millions of invoice and booking-line records, and investigate records that do not reconcile.

  • 02

    Processing tools

    I build AI-assisted processing agents, exception and reconciliation tools, and interfaces for process analysis.

  • 03

    Python workflows

    I rebuild legacy Alteryx transformations in Python so recurring processing logic can be maintained in code.
